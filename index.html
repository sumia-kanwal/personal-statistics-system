Profile Questionnaire

An online questionnaire that collects personal statistical information from participants and stores each submission as a structured record for descriptive research.

Open questionnaire
Initial dataset

The dataset starts empty. No sample or generated research data is included. Responses stay in this browser and are not uploaded to a server. Charts and averages appear only after real submissions.

What is collected

Background

Name, age, gender, nationality, education, marital status, student/employee status, hobbies, and country of residence.

Physical measures

Height in centimetres and weight in kilograms, used for average height and weight on the dashboard.

Daily habits

Typical daily sleeping hours and daily exercise duration in minutes.

How the system works

  1. A participant completes and submits the validated questionnaire.
  2. The browser saves that response as one record in localStorage.
  3. The administrator dashboard shows counts, averages, distributions, a record table, and CSV export.
  4. Optional: export the CSV and analyse it with the included C++ statistics program.
